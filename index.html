FROM THE PRINCIPAL

ROBOKRITI represents the spirit of learning by doing. When students design, test and compete, they learn not only how machines work, but how ideas become real through discipline, collaboration and resilience. I wish every participating team confidence, creativity and a memorable experience in the arena.

FROM THE MENTOR

Robotics grows when students are given space to experiment, fail safely, rethink and try again. ROBOKRITI is designed as that space — a place where technical thinking meets teamwork and every run teaches something new. Build with curiosity, compete with sportsmanship and let your robot tell the story of your engineering.

AKANSHA RANI · THINKING AND ROBOTICS MENTOR
THE MISSION

Engineering is a team sport.

No ordinary brochure-style experience. ROBOKRITI is a digital arena built around the competition itself — dynamic information, live status, registration workflows and a control room for organizers.
