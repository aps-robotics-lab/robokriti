ABOUT ROBOKRITI

THE EVENT
BEHIND THE ARENA.

ROBOKRITI 2026 is an inter-class robotics championship at Army Public School, Lal Bahadur Shastri Marg, Lucknow. The event brings students into a practical environment where engineering, strategy, creativity and teamwork meet competition.

EVENT DATE07 SEP 2026
REGISTRATION CLOSES03 SEP · 11:59 PM
ELIGIBILITYCLASSES 6–12
VENUEAPS LBS MARG

“Our students learn best when curiosity has a place to become action.”

SADHANA DEVI · PRINCIPAL
PRINCIPAL MESSAGE

At Army Public School, we encourage students to approach learning with curiosity, responsibility and confidence. ROBOKRITI creates an exciting space where classroom concepts can become working machines and where teamwork is tested in a real competitive environment. I hope every participant embraces the challenge, learns from every attempt and represents the school with creativity and sportsmanship.

MENTOR MESSAGE

Thinking like a roboticist means being willing to ask why, test what happens, observe failure and redesign. ROBOKRITI is more than a competition; it is a learning loop. Build carefully, understand your machine, support your teammates and keep improving. The strongest teams are not always the ones that start perfect — they are the ones that learn fastest.

AKANSHA RANI · THINKING AND ROBOTICS MENTOR
