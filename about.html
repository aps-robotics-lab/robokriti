ABOUT ROBOKRITI

Built for the makers.

ROBOKRITI 2026 is an inter-class robotics championship designed around hands-on engineering, machine control and team problem-solving.

THE IDEA

Not just a race.
A robotics culture.

The championship gives students a competitive environment to take ideas from the tinkering bench into a live arena. The visual system reflects that mindset: dark technical surfaces, editorial typography, machine imagery and controlled motion.

ORGANIZING CONTEXT

APS Lucknow · Tinkering Lab

ROBOKRITI is presented through the Tinkering Lab at Army Public School, Lal Bahadur Shastri Marg, Lucknow. The championship experience is designed to celebrate student-built robotics and responsible competition.
