Principal

SADHANA
DEVI.

Principal · Army Public School, Lal Bahadur Shastri Marg

“ROBOKRITI is a celebration of curiosity, teamwork and the courage to build something of your own. I hope every participant approaches the arena not simply to win, but to experiment, learn from every attempt and discover the confidence that comes from turning an idea into a working machine. May this competition inspire our students to keep asking better questions and creating bolder solutions.”

“Robotics teaches us that failure is not a full stop—it is data. ROBOKRITI gives students a space to think with their hands, test their decisions and collaborate under pressure. Build fearlessly, observe carefully and keep improving your machine one iteration at a time. The best part of the arena is not knowing exactly what will happen next.”
