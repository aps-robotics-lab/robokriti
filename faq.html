SYSTEM FAQ

Questions before deployment.

Quick answers to the questions teams are most likely to ask.

Who can participate?
Students from Classes 6–12 can participate. Team members may come from different classes and sections.
How many students can be in a team?
A team can have 1 to 5 students. The team leader is counted as Member 1.
Is registration free?
Yes. Registration is free.
When does registration close?
Registration closes on 03 September 2026 at the stated deadline. The website and Firebase submission logic enforce the deadline.
Can students from different sections form a team?
Yes. Students can be from different classes and sections.
Is Robo Soccer a full competition?
Robo Soccer is currently marked as a demo event. Its final format can be communicated through announcements.
Where can I check an enquiry?
After submitting a contact enquiry, save the RB ticket reference. The Contact page lets you check its public status.
Where will official updates appear?
Use the Announcements page. The organizing team can publish updates from the admin dashboard.
