Who can participate?

Students from Classes 6 to 12 can participate. Teams may include members from different classes and sections.

How many students can be on a team?

A maximum of five students, including the team leader. The leader counts as the first member.

When does registration close?

Registration closes on 03 September 2026 at 11:59 PM IST.

When is the competition?

The competition takes place on 07 September 2026.

Is registration free?

Yes. Registration is free.

Can students from different sections form one team?

Yes. Team members may come from different classes and sections, subject to the eligibility rules.

Is Robo Soccer competitive?

Robo Soccer is currently presented as a demo/exhibition event.
