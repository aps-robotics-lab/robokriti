01 / CORE RULES

THE ARENA
HAS RULES.

Who can participate?

Students from Classes 6 to 12. Team members may come from different classes and sections.

Maximum team size?

Up to 5 students including the team leader.

Safety?

Follow organizer instructions, keep clear of restricted areas and use equipment responsibly. Unsafe machines may be stopped.

Late arrival?

Teams should report according to the schedule communicated by organizers. Late arrival may affect participation.
