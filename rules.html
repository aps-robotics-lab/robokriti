COMPETITION PROTOCOL

Rules before robots.

The public rulebook below is the baseline. Event-specific instructions and safety calls communicated by the organizing team take precedence.

01 · ELIGIBILITY

Who can compete?

Students from Classes 6 to 12 are eligible. Teams may combine students from different classes and sections. A team may contain up to 5 members, including the team leader.

Registration is free and closes on 03 September 2026.

02 · ROBOT & SAFETY

Build responsibly.

01

Pre-check

Robots may be inspected before entering an arena.

02

Safe operation

Operators must follow organizer instructions and stop immediately when instructed.

03

Arena discipline

Only the designated team members/operators may participate during a match.

04

Decisions

Event officials' decisions during the competition are final for that round.

03 · EVENT-SPECIFIC

Know your arena.

RACE

Course control and completion take priority over reckless speed.

TUG

Traction, control and safe machine interaction define the arena.

WAR

Controlled competition only; safety instructions always override tactical play.
