UNIVERSAL

Core competition principles

Eligibility

ROBOKRITI is for Classes 6–12. Event rulebooks may define Junior and Senior categories differently, so teams must check the event-specific document.

Safety

No dangerous mechanisms, exposed unsafe electrical systems, fire, explosives or intentional arena damage.

Fair play

Only registered team members participate. Follow officials, respect competitors and accept final decisions.

Inspection

Robots may be inspected before and during competition. Teams must meet the dimensions, weight, voltage and safety requirements of their event.
